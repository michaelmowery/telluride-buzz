About {{site}}

{{site}} is the real-time pulse of {{town}}: what's happening in our town, right now, from sources you can trust.

Instead of checking a dozen websites before you head out, open one page. Weather, roads, the mountain and local news are pulled together automatically, and every post links back to where it came from.

Preview: {{site}} is in early testing. Information may be wrong or out of date, so check the original source before you rely on it. It is not an emergency service: in an emergency, call 911.

Where the information comes from

{{aboutSources}}
SourceWhat we showChecked

"Checked" is how often we ask the source for updates. A source only changes when its publisher posts something new, so a post can be older than that. Each tile shows how long ago its data was updated, and turns red when it is out of date.

We don't write or edit the news. Titles and summaries come from the source, and alerts are shown as the agency issued them.

Alerts on your phone

Tap Turn on alerts on the home page to get a notification for winter storm warnings, road closures and county emergencies. On iPhone, first add {{site}} to your Home Screen (Share, then Add to Home Screen). We store only the anonymous address your browser gives us to deliver the alert. No account, no email, no tracking.

Spot a problem?

If something is wrong, missing or out of date, please tell us. Include what you saw and roughly when.
